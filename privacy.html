Privacy

DON'T PRESS THAT is designed to work without creating a player account. The game does not ask you to sign in before playing and does not require you to submit a name, email address, phone number or payment information.

Local browser storage

The game can save limited information in your browser, such as your best score, number of runs and sound preference. This information is stored locally on your device using browser storage. It is used to remember gameplay preferences and progress on that browser. You can remove it by clearing site data in your browser.

Technical hosting data

Like most websites, the hosting and delivery infrastructure may process routine technical information required to serve pages, such as request timestamps, IP addresses, browser details and security-related logs. This information may be handled by the services used to host and deliver the website according to their own operational and security practices.

Music and external resources

Background music may be delivered from an external content host after you interact with the game. Loading an external resource can cause your browser to connect to that provider. The game also contains optional links to other websites. Visiting those sites is a separate interaction governed by their own privacy policies.

Cookies and advertising

The game itself does not require advertising cookies or an advertising account to function. If analytics, advertising or other data-collecting services are added in the future, this page should be updated before those services are used.

Children and sensitive information

The game does not intentionally request sensitive personal information. Players should avoid sending personal information through any external service unless they understand that service's privacy terms.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the website's features, storage practices, hosting arrangements or third-party services change.
