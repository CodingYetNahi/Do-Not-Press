About & How to Play

DON'T PRESS THAT is a browser-based collection of quick brain games and mini challenges. Each round gives you a short instruction and a limited amount of time. Your goal is to understand the rule, act accurately and build the longest possible streak without failing a challenge.

What kinds of games are included?

The challenge deck mixes memory, path recall, number patterns, logic, visual attention, direction changes, timing, counting, reaction control and other short mental tasks. Follow the Path, for example, shows a route across a group of tiles and then asks you to reproduce that route from memory. Other rounds ask you to solve sequences, identify an odd symbol, compare values or respond only when a cue appears.

Scoring

Each successfully completed challenge adds one point. A failed challenge ends the current run. Your best score is stored locally in your browser so you can compare future runs on the same device.

Controls

The game is designed for touch screens as well as desktop browsers. Tap, swipe, drag or choose an answer depending on the current instruction. Sound can be muted at any time. Opening the menu during a run pauses the active challenge and resumes it safely when the menu is closed.
